TITLE
Pramana: a tutor that can only say what your course says | Multimodal AI Hackathon 2026, Track D

DESCRIPTION
Pramana (Sanskrit: proof) reads a course's textbook, slide decks and lecture videos, then tutors, tests and teaches a student from that material alone. Every sentence of an answer carries a number that opens the textbook page with the passage outlined, the slide, or the lecture at the second it was said. If the course does not cover a question, it says so.
