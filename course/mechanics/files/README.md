# Slide decks shipped with the repository

PHYS 2425 Lecture Slides, chapters 3, 5, 6 and 7, by the Collin College Physics Department
(adapted from OpenStax *University Physics*), from https://digitalcommons.collin.edu/physics/.
Licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The files are
unchanged.

They are included so that `make fetch` works without a browser: their host now answers
scripted downloads with a browser check. `make fetch` copies them from here; the textbook
and the lecture videos are still downloaded from their publishers.
